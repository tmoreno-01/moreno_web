Hey there, I'm Tyrone Doyle Moreno, a half-Spanish, half-English artist who delves into creating uninhibited art in an attempt to deal with my extreme ADHD.

Creating is pure catharsis for me. Regardless of the medium, be it pixels or mashing paint onto a canvas, it’s what makes me feel human; the flow state of creating is the closest we get to dreaming whilst awake.
